Large language model agents answer financial questions by searching regulatory filings, but a well-grounded number can reflect the wrong interpretation. Meta Platforms' operating income, for example, is \$46.75B consolidated or \$62.87B for its Family of Apps segment. A capable agent should clarify which reading the user intends. We introduce \fininteract, a bilingual English/Chinese benchmark of 173 instances with paired default and intended interpretations, organized by a five-category ambiguity taxonomy. It evaluates whether agents elicit useful clarification and integrate it into their final answer. Re-grading identical retrieval-assisted outputs against the default rather than intended reading changes scores substantially: GPT-4o receives approximately three times the credit in aggregate, although the direction reverses on the English split. The evaluated GPT models reach 93--95\% accuracy with the intended context and evidence supplied, compared with at most 20.2\% under free interaction. Category targeting alone does not establish that a question resolves ambiguity. Category-guided supervision improves Qwen3-14B's interaction accuracy from 32.4\% to 60.1\%, with gains concentrated in Chinese. We release the benchmark and evaluation code.
