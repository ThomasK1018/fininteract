We develop four implications in Appendix~\ref{app:discussion}. \textbf{Category-level reporting matters}: aggregate scores conceal uneven targeting and resolution. \textbf{Clarification behavior can be trained}, although our strongest end-to-end gain is concentrated in Chinese and requires broader replication. \textbf{Human elicitation also remains difficult} under the single-question study protocol; its distinct subset prevents a direct ranking against the model runs. Finally, \textbf{paired interpretations offer a construction strategy} for further categories, languages, and domains, whose validity would require new domain-specific evaluation.
