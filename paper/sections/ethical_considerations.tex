\fininteract is intended for research on clarification and intent integration, not as financial advice or evidence that an agent is ready for autonomous financial decisions. A plausible answer to the wrong interpretation can mislead downstream analysis; users should verify both the intended scope and supporting evidence. The benchmark uses public corporate disclosures, and quoted source material retains its original rights and terms. Its small, language-skewed sample and constructed user contexts limit conclusions about real users and deployment. Annotation procedures and compensation are described in Appendix~\ref{app:construction}.
