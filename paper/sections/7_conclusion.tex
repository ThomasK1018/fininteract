We introduced \fininteract, a bilingual benchmark for clarification and intent integration in ambiguous financial QA. Its paired default and intended interpretations expose how grading against one defensible reading can miss failure to recover another. The evaluated models often target a relevant category without resolving the ambiguity, and supplying the intended context and evidence substantially improves accuracy. Category-guided supervision improves Qwen3-14B's end-to-end accuracy, chiefly on the Chinese split. The benchmark remains small, language-skewed, and restricted to ambiguous questions; progress on it does not establish a calibrated when-to-ask policy. We release the frozen evaluation snapshot, a corrected dataset version, and the research code to support reproducible follow-on work.
